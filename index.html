A Shopify theme for furniture, interiors and design objects. This guide explains the native editor and the theme's original sections.

Pre-release documentation. The theme has not been accepted or listed on Shopify Theme Store. Release availability will be announced after review.

01 / Getting started

  1. When the theme becomes available, install it through Shopify Theme Store. Keep a duplicate of your existing theme before switching.
  2. Open Online Store → Themes → Customize on your unpublished theme.
  3. Select a page template from the editor menu. Add sections, change their order and save your changes.
  4. Set your logo, navigation menus, fonts and color schemes. Preview the store with your actual products and content before publishing.

The theme uses Shopify products, collections, pages, blogs and navigation. No paid app is required for the built-in sections. Demo photographs and products are not included.

02 / Make it your own

Editorial home

Use Editorial hero for your main image, heading, caption and collection link. Add Manifesto for a short statement. Featured collection uses a collection selected from your own catalog.

Shop by room

Choose a room image, add a product block, select a product and adjust its horizontal and vertical position. The visible product links also provide access to each item without relying on the image hotspots. Remove references to deleted products.

Products and object details

Reorder title, price, description, purchase and detail blocks. Add dimensions, materials and care content in rich text; use the editor's dynamic-source button when a compatible source is available. Empty detail content is hidden. App blocks depend on the app you install.

Collections, search and cart

Product availability, prices, inventory and media come from Shopify. Configure storefront filters in Shopify's native filtering tools. Cart updates and checkout use Shopify. The theme does not control payment approval, taxes or shipping rules.

Contact, blog and protected stores

Assign the contact template to a Shopify page for the native contact form. Blog and article pages use Shopify content and comment settings. The password page uses your existing storefront password; it does not create or change credentials.

Gift cards and languages

Gift cards use Shopify-issued balances, codes and validity. Copy and print are optional conveniences; the code stays selectable without JavaScript. Apple Wallet is shown only when Shopify provides a pass. English and Spanish theme controls are included; translate your own catalog and text through Shopify's localization tools.

03 / Troubleshooting

  • Missing products: check the selected collection, Online Store publication and product availability.
  • Missing image: select a merchant image in the section settings. Placeholder artwork is shown when a compatible image is absent.
  • Cannot add a variant: check inventory, the complete selected option and Shopify quantity rules.
  • Changes are invisible: save the editor and confirm you are previewing the intended theme.
  • Checkout or payment issue: verify Shopify payment, market, shipping and tax configuration. Never send us a password or card number.

Before reporting a problem, reproduce it in a duplicate theme and note whether a recently installed app or custom code is involved. Do not remove live-store apps merely to test.

Guía en español

Galeria Cromatica es un tema para muebles, interiores y objetos de diseño. Todavía está en preparación: no ha sido aceptado ni publicado en Shopify Theme Store.

Instalación y personalización

  1. Cuando esté disponible, instala el tema desde Shopify Theme Store y conserva una copia del tema anterior.
  2. En Tienda online → Temas → Personalizar, configura menús, tipografías y esquemas de color.
  3. En la portada editorial, elige una imagen, título, leyenda y enlace. Añade Manifiesto y Colección destacada según tu catálogo.
  4. En Compra por ambiente, elige una foto y añade bloques de producto con coordenadas horizontales y verticales. Los enlaces visibles permiten acceder a cada producto.
  5. En producto, organiza título, precio, descripción, compra y detalles. Usa fuentes dinámicas compatibles para medidas, materiales y cuidados. Los detalles vacíos se ocultan.
  6. Asigna la plantilla contact a una página para usar el formulario nativo de Shopify. Blog, comentarios, contraseña y tarjetas regalo utilizan las funciones nativas de Shopify.

No necesitas una app de pago para las secciones incluidas. Las fotografías y productos de la demo no se entregan con el tema. Las traducciones de interfaz son ES/EN; el contenido de tu catálogo se traduce por separado.

Problemas frecuentes

Si no aparecen productos, revisa la colección elegida y su publicación en Tienda online. Para variantes que no se pueden comprar, comprueba inventario y reglas de cantidad. Guarda los cambios y confirma qué tema estás previsualizando. Los pagos, impuestos y envíos se configuran en Shopify.
